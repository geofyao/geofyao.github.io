This page describes the variables required to calculate the impacts of particulate matter (PM) on solar photovoltaic (PV) efficiency or capacity factor, which are typically expressed as unitless quantities. To calculate the impacts of fine particulate matter (PM$_{2.5}$) on public health, only PM$_{2.5}$ concentrations, typically expressed in $\mu g~m^{-3}$, are needed.
