This page describes the variables required to calculate the impacts of particulate matter (PM) on solar photovoltaic (PV) efficiency or capacity factor (CF), which are typically expressed as unitless quantities. To calculate the impacts of fine particulate matter (PM<sub>2.5</sub>) on public health, only PM<sub>2.5</sub> concentrations, typically expressed in µg m<sup>-3</sup>, are needed.

**Each data record <u>must</u> contain coordinates for latitude, longitude, and time, as well as the following variables:**

GHI: Global Horizontal Irradiance typically expressed in W m<sup>-2</sup>

**Each data record <u>may</u> include the following optional variables:**

DNI: Direct Normal Irridance typically expressed in W m<sup>-2</sup>

DHI: Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance typically expressed in W m<sup>-2</sup>
